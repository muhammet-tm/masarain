Simulation

One voice call, one route, two ways of handling a network that fades.

Compared withSwitch after failure

The prototype is real software: a server, two clients, and authenticated packets. Only the radio links are emulated; they replay the link traces of the project's simulator. Each recording is a short stretch of a scenario, chosen as an illustration, so its figures differ from the averages over the full scenarios, which are on the overview page under Results.

Keys: Space plays or pauses, the arrow keys step one second (five with Shift), R restarts, and the number keys pick a route.
